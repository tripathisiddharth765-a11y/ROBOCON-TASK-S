What It Does

Assignemnt 4 -
A C++ program that processes sensor input from a line-following robot and determines its next action. Essential for robotics projects like your Rudra club work!

What It Does

Reads 8 sensor inputs and calculates the robot's position relative to a line, then commands the appropriate movement:

Line Lost — no sensors detecting the line
Turn Left — robot is too far right
Turn Right — robot is too far left
Move Forward — robot is centered on the line
