An Arduino sketch that controls two servos with a single potentiometer — one spins forward while the other spins backward, creating a synchronized mirror effect. Perfect for robotic arms, pan-tilt systems, or learning servo control!

What It Does

Uses a potentiometer to control two servos in opposite directions:

Turn dial left → Servo1 goes to 0°, Servo2 goes to 180°
Turn dial middle → Servo1 at 90°, Servo2 at 90° (crossed)
Turn dial right → Servo1 goes to 180°, Servo2 goes to 0°
