Assignment 1 - 
A lightweight C++ utility for analyzing real-time sensor readings. Perfect for quick prototyping in embedded systems and IoT projects.

What It Does

Takes a batch of sensor readings and gives you the key stats:

Max & Min values — the highest and lowest readings
Above/Below thresholds — counts readings that exceed 100 or fall below 20
Average — the mean of all readings

Assignment 2 -
A simple C++ tool that counts how many times each digit appears in a number. Great for understanding digit distribution and frequency analysis.

What It Does

Breaks down a number and tells you:

How many times each digit (0-9) appears
Only shows digits that actually exist in your number (keeps it clean)
